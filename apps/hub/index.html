Why Turon

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity shouldn’t need a bus ticket.

Turon was founded in June 2024 at the Presidential School in Fergana as FerganaPS MUN. It became TuronMUN in 2025, and Turon Debate followed in November 2026. It is still run by students.

Our goal is simple: young people in the region shouldn’t have to move to another city to find serious opportunities.

FAQ

Can’t find an answer? Write to us on Telegram or by email.

Who can take part?

School and university students. No experience needed: most TuronMUN delegates came to their first conference with us.

Does it cost anything?

TuronMUN has a participation fee that depends on your delegation type, with early-bird discounts. Turon Debate announces its terms on its own page.

What language is it in?

TuronMUN runs mainly in English, with Uzbek and Russian committees in some seasons. Each Turon Debate session announces its language.

Where do I hear about new events?

Follow our Telegram and Instagram. Announcements and registration links go there first.

How can my organisation partner with Turon?

Email admin@turonmun.com: event sponsorship, a prize or scholarship, a workshop or mentoring. We’ll pick the format together.
