How it works

Three phases, one paper

It opens the door wide first, then narrows, like the T in our logo.

  1. Open to many

    Research School

    Workshops on the basics. Worth it even if you stop here.

    • Asking a good question
    • Finding and reading sources
    • Collecting data honestly, and writing it up
    • A certificate when you finish
  2. Your proposal

    Competition

    Judged like a research fair, not a test.

    • A short research proposal
    • A short talk, then questions
    • Scored by judges against a published rubric
  3. 5–6 finalists

    Research Programme

    Months of real work with university mentors, online.

    • Weekly work with your mentor
    • A finished paper with your name on it
    • Submitted to a student research journal

Who can join and what you get

Who can join

  • School students from anywhere in the Fergana region
  • No research experience needed: that's what the School is for
  • Curious about anything: science, society, economics, health, the environment

What you get

  • Research skills and a certificate from the School
  • Practice pitching your idea to real judges
  • For finalists: a mentor, a finished paper and a journal submission
  • Free. Merit-based, never pay-to-play.
  • Teaches first. You don't have to shine already to get in.
  • Student-led. Run by young people in Fergana, like the rest of Turon.

FAQ

Questions

Something else? Ask on Telegram or by email.

Does it cost anything?

No. Every phase is free.

Do I need to have done research before?

No. The Research School teaches the basics from the start. That's the point of it.

Will my paper be published?

Finalists finish a paper and submit it to a student research journal. Journals make their own decisions, so we can't promise publication, and we only work with honest, free journals, never pay-to-publish ones.

When does it start?

Dates aren't set yet. Follow our Telegram to hear first when the Research School opens.

Who runs Turon Research?

Turon, a student-led youth ecosystem in Fergana. It's also behind TuronMUN, Turon Debate and Vibe Turon.
